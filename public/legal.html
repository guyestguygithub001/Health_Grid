2. Privacy Policy

Effective Date: [Date]

1. Introduction

Health Grid ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal and medical data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect data in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA).

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Data: Names, email addresses, and phone numbers of healthcare professionals using our platform.

Under the NDPA, patients have the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of their data. As Health Grid is a Data Processor, patients must direct these requests to their healthcare provider (the Data Controller).

2. Processor Obligations
